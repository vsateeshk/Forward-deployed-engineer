Customer Return Policy
1. All online purchases can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund to the original payment method.
2. Items must be in unused condition and include the original packaging when possible.
3. Electronics are eligible for return within 15 days only unless the item is defective.
4. Electronics that are returned after 15 days are subject to a 15% restocking fee.
5. Defective electronics may be exchanged or refunded without the restocking fee.
6. VIP members receive free return shipping on eligible items.
7. Non-VIP customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the return is due to a defect or shipping error.
8. Orders over $500 require manager review before any return is approved.
9. Gift cards and personalized items are non-returnable.
10. Final sale and clearance items are not eligible for returns.
11. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected.
12. If a customer reports a damaged product, the case should be reviewed for replacement or refund eligibility.
13. Store credit may be offered instead of a cash refund for certain promotional orders.
14. If the return request conflicts with policy, the case should be escalated to the customer support manager.
15. Management approval is required for exceptions, high-value cases, or any return decision that falls outside standard policy rules.
